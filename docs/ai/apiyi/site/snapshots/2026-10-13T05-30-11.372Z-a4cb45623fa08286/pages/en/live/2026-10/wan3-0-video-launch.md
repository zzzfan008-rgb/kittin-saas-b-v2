# Wan3.0 Video Generation Now Live

> Alibaba Tongyi Wanxiang Wan3.0 is live in two variants, wan3.0-video (Standard) and wan3.0-video-prime (Prime). One model ID covers text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-to-video and video editing, up to 30 seconds, 480P–1080P, with audio by default; 98% of official price, $0.0588/sec for Standard at 720P.

**2026/10/10 22:23 (UTC+8)** · New Model · Alibaba

🚀 **Wan3.0 video generation is live: `wan3.0-video` and `wan3.0-video-prime`**

Wan2.7 makes you switch between four model IDs (`t2v` / `i2v` / `r2v` / `videoedit`). Wan3.0 handles text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-to-video and video editing through a single model ID, and the `type` of each `input.media[]` item decides the mode. Clips run up to 30 seconds, a new 480P tier is available, and output includes an audio track by default.

* `wan3.0-video` (Standard): \$0.0588/sec at 720P, about 30% cheaper than Wan2.7
* `wan3.0-video-prime` (Prime): \$0.126/sec at 720P, faster delivery, same parameters as Standard

Pricing is 98% of Alibaba Cloud's official rate by default, and as low as about 82% with the top tier of top-up bonuses. Create your token with the `Wan&HappyHorse` group and usage-based billing. Wan2.7 remains available. See the [Wan3.0 Video Generation overview](/en/api-capabilities/wan3/overview); the [Model Pricing](/en/models/index) page is authoritative for current prices.
