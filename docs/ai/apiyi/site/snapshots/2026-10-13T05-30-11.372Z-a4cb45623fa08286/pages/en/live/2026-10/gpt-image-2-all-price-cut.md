# gpt-image-2-all and gpt-image-2.5-all cut to $0.025 per image

**2026/10/10 14:05 (UTC+8)** · Price Update · OpenAI

💸 **`gpt-image-2-all` and `gpt-image-2.5-all` price cut: \$0.03 → \$0.025 per image**

The -all series (ChatGPT web line) delivers high image quality on its own and is more stable than the -vip series, so we have lowered its price to make it the easy choice for everyday generation. Its only trade-off is 1K–1.5K output — no 2K / 4K — which covers routine work.

* `gpt-image-2-all` / `gpt-image-2.5-all`: \$0.025 per image, per-call billing, no change to how you call them
* `gpt-image-2-vip` / `gpt-image-2.5-flare-vip` / `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-vip`: price unchanged (\$0.03 per image), with locked sizes and 4K; more exposed to provider-side risk controls, and we keep maintaining them and adding capacity

\$0.025 is the pre-discount price — stack it with the top-up bonus (10%–20% extra credit) and the effective cost is lower still. See the [GPT-Image-2.5-All overview](/en/api-capabilities/gpt-image-2-all/overview); the [Model Pricing](/en/models/index) tab is authoritative for current prices.
