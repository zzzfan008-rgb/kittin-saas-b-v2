# Monthly Bill Export Service

> Need last month's usage bill? Ask in your dedicated WeCom group and we will deliver it after the 8th of each month, or submit a ticket on the site and we will take care of it.

**2026/10/5 00:22 (UTC+8)** · Service Notice

🧾 **Need last month's bill? We can export it for you**

Bills are issued monthly, and last month's bill is delivered after the 8th of each month. You can request it in either of two ways:

* Post your request in your dedicated WeCom group
* Submit a ticket on the site

We'll take good care of you.
