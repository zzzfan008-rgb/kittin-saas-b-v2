<Update label="Oct 12, 2026 · Personal Access Tokens Are Live" description="Service Notice">
  ### Personal Access Tokens Are Live

  **Legacy system tokens are scheduled to retire on 2026-11-11 (UTC+8)**

  Balance, log and token management APIs now use personal access tokens, which support per-scope permissions, expiry dates and IP allowlists. Legacy system tokens are scheduled to retire on 2026-11-11 (UTC+8). We may extend the date based on usage, but recommend switching now: just replace the header value.

  📖 [Read details](/en/live/2026-10/personal-access-token-launch) | ❓ [Personal access token guide](/en/faq/personal-access-token)
</Update>
