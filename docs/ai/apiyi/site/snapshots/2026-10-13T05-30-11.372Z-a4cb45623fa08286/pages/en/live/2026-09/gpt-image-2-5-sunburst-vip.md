# gpt-image-2.5 -vip Models Replenished and Available Again

> As of 23 September, the reverse models gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-vip and gpt-image-2.5-flare-vip have had capacity added and are available again, closing the 15 September supply-gap notice. Both run on the Adobe Firefly reverse route with per-request billing; model names and usage are unchanged, so you can call them directly.

**2026/9/23 12:19 (UTC+8)** · Model Status · OpenAI

✅ **`gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-vip` and `gpt-image-2.5-flare-vip` have had capacity added and are available again**

Both models run on the Adobe Firefly reverse route (the `gpt-image-2.5-vip` alias points to `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-vip`) and were temporarily unavailable due to tight provider-side capacity. Capacity has now been added, and the 15 September supply-gap notice is closed. Model names, endpoints and per-request billing are unchanged; see the [GPT-Image-2-VIP Overview](/en/api-capabilities/gpt-image-2-vip/overview) for parameters and usage.

Go ahead and use them 🚀 If you hit a sporadic failure, simply retry; we are monitoring continuously.
