# New Claude_Enterprise Group (1.2x Rate)

> New Claude_Enterprise group (identifier ClaudeOfficial) at a 1.2x rate. Anthropic official channel only, no AWS, with a high cache hit rate and 12 Claude models; priced on the same basis as image2Enterprise.

**2026/10/7 15:51 (UTC+8)** · New Model · Anthropic

🚀 **New `Claude_Enterprise` group (identifier `ClaudeOfficial`) at a 1.2x rate**

Launched for enterprise customers. It **runs only on the Anthropic official channel, with no AWS Claude**, has a high cache hit rate, and puts stability first. Rate calculation: conversion rate 1.2 × group rate 1 × recharge rate 1 = **1.2x**.

Pricing follows the same basis as the [`image2Enterprise` enterprise group](/en/live/2026-04/new-image2-enterprise-group): 1.2x is set so that a \$3,000 single recharge with the 20% bonus lands at roughly the official list price. We absorb tax costs and prioritize supply over margin.

12 models are supported:

* Fable: `claude-fable-5`, `claude-fable-5-1`
* Opus: `claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-opus-5`, `claude-opus-4-8`, `claude-opus-4-7`, `claude-opus-4-6`, `claude-opus-4-5-20251101`
* Sonnet: `claude-sonnet-5-5`, `claude-sonnet-5`, `claude-sonnet-4-6`
* Haiku: `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`

Each model also has a `-thinking` alias (e.g. `claude-opus-5-5-thinking`) for reasoning chats in OpenAI-compatible mode. In most cases use the model name without `-thinking` and control reasoning through request parameters.

To use it, select `Claude_Enterprise` as the group in your token settings. Available groups and models are listed in the [user center](https://api.apiyi.com/account/profile).

Fable 5 and Fable 5.1 are Mythos-class models: inputs and outputs are retained for 30 days on Anthropic's side for abuse detection. APIYI itself does not retain any data.
