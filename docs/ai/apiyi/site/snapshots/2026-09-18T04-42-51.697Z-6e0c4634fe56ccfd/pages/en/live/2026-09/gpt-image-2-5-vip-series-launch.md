# gpt-image-2.5-vip Reverse Series Is Live, gpt-image-2.5-all Alongside

> Three new reverse-engineered models: gpt-image-2.5-vip (alias of gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-vip), gpt-image-2.5-flare-vip and gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-vip, on the same Adobe reverse line as gpt-image-2-vip at $0.03 per image, Default group, unchanged call format. All three accept quality up to high and support transparent backgrounds, with 30 locked sizes. gpt-image-2.5-all ships alongside, same price and behavior as gpt-image-2-all.

**2026/9/9 19:55 (UTC+8)** · New Model · OpenAI

🚀 **The `gpt-image-2.5-vip` reverse series is live and callable in the `Default` group**

Three new models: `gpt-image-2.5-vip` (alias of `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-vip`), `gpt-image-2.5-flare-vip` (speed-first) and `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-vip` (quality- and editing-first). They run on the same Adobe reverse line as `gpt-image-2-vip` (Firefly, a high-quality GPT-Image 2.5 reverse line, not low-quality upscaling) with **the same \$0.03 per-image price, groups, endpoints and call format**; just swap the `model` name.

A three-arm comparison on the same channel and token before launch showed all three accept `quality` up to `high` (`xhigh` / `max` unavailable), support `background: "transparent"`, and lock all 30 `size` presets pixel-exact. Note that 2.5 `high` only equals `gpt-image-2-vip` `medium`, so do not carry `quality` over verbatim when migrating; `mask` is whole-image regeneration on all three, so use the official models for precise inpainting.

On the ChatGPT web reverse line, `gpt-image-2.5-all` ships alongside: the ChatGPT web app has moved to Images 2.5 as a whole, so `gpt-image-2-all` already produces 2.5 images; the new name simply makes the upgrade explicit, and the two names share price and behavior.

The docs sidebar groups are now "GPT-Image-2.5-VIP Image Gen" and "GPT-Image-2.5-All Image Gen"; the old model pages and URLs are unchanged.
