# Nano Banana 2/Pro Supply Paused · 2K/4K Blurry in Google Outage

> Due to a Google-side outage, the 2K/4K output of Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image) and Nano Banana 2 (gemini-3.1-flash-image) only comes back blurry, which would waste credits, so we're temporarily pausing supply of both models; we'll restore it once we verify Google has recovered.

**2026/6/19 15:46 (UTC+8)** · Service Notice · Google

⏸ **Nano Banana 2 / Pro supply paused · 2K/4K only return blurry images, avoiding wasted spend**

Due to a Google-side outage, the 2K / 4K output of `gemini-3-pro-image` (Nano Banana Pro) and `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (Nano Banana 2) currently comes back blurry (see [Nano Banana 2K/4K blurry](/en/live/2026-06/nano-banana-4k-2k-blurry)). Since continued calls would waste credits, we are temporarily pausing supply of these two models.

We're testing and watching the official status, and will restore supply the moment Google recovers. Sorry for the trouble — this is an issue on Google's side.
