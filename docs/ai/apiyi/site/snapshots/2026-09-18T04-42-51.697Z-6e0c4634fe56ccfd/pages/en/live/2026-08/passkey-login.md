# Passkey Sign-In Is Live: Log In With a Fingerprint, No Password to Remember

> APIYI now supports passkey sign-in. Bind one from Account Options at the bottom of your Profile page, pick where to save it, and log in with a fingerprint, face scan, or screen lock. The private key stays on your device, making it phishing- and credential-stuffing-resistant; the last-used date is shown and you can unbind anytime.

**2026/8/1 00:24 (UTC+8)** · Service Notice

🚀 **Passkey sign-in is live — bind one and a fingerprint or face scan gets you into the console, no password to remember**

How to bind: sign in, open **Profile**, scroll to **Account Options** at the bottom of the page, click **Bind Passkey**, and choose where to save it in the system dialog (iCloud Keychain / Google Password Manager / phone, tablet or security key / this browser profile).

The security comes from the mechanism itself: the private key stays in your device or password manager, and the server holds only a public key from which the private key cannot be derived. That makes passkeys phishing- and credential-stuffing-resistant — a look-alike site cannot trigger verification at all. Fingerprint and face data are verified locally on your device and never uploaded.

Once bound, the same spot shows the **last used date**, and you can **unbind** at any time — unbinding does not affect your balance, tokens, or usage records.

Two reminders: keep a backup sign-in method available (password / registered email / GitHub) so a lost device is recoverable, and do not bind on a shared computer — for temporary access, use the "phone, tablet, or security key" option and verify by QR code on your own phone.
