# Referral Rebate 5% Policy + Reward Transfer Workflow FAQ Published

> APIYI has no separate agent partnership channel — the referral rebate program is open to all users: 5% continuous rebate (adjusted from 10% on 5/15), uniform for both enterprise and individual users. Rewards must be manually transferred to your main balance before they can be spent; filter logs by 奖励金划转 (Reward Transfer) to see every payout. Self-invitation farming is strictly prohibited.

**2026/5/18 21:06 (UTC+8)** · Docs Update

🎉 **Referral Rebate 5% Policy + Reward Transfer Workflow FAQ Published** — New FAQ at [/en/faq/referral-program](/en/faq/referral-program): grab your invite code / invite link from the **推广信息 (Referral Info)** section in the [Account Center](https://api.apiyi.com/account/profile), and earn a **continuous 5% credit rebate** on every invitee's recharges (adjusted from 10% to 5% starting 5/15, **same rate for enterprise and individual users**). Rewards need to be **manually transferred** to your main balance before they can be spent — filter the logs by **奖励金划转 (Reward Transfer)** to see every payout.

⚠️ **Anti-abuse notice**: Self-invitation farming (using a main account to invite your own alts) is strictly prohibited. The platform identifies suspicious accounts via **IP / device fingerprint / payment info**, and **reserves the right to cancel the referral relationship, reclaim or freeze rebates**, and escalate to account risk-control actions when warranted.
