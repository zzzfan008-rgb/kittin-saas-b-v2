# What Is APIYI's Refund Policy?

> Learn about APIYI's refund conditions, process, fees, and invoice-related considerations

## Quick Answer

APIYI offers a **30-day no-questions-asked refund policy** after top-up. Unused balance is refunded to the original payment method. Promotional bonus credits are deducted from the refund. WeChat and Alipay refunds are fee-free; other payment methods incur a 1% processing fee. Note also that, effective 2 August 2026, account balances carry a **365-day validity period** — expired balance is not refunded or restored, see [balance validity](/en/faq/recharge-promotions).

<Info>
  **Refund Promise**: We want every customer to top up with confidence. Within 30 days, if you're not satisfied, you can request a refund anytime — unused balance is fully returned.
</Info>

## Refund Calculation

Refund amount = Top-up amount - Used amount - Bonus credits - Processing fee (if applicable)

**Example**:

Say you topped up \$100, received a \$10 promotional bonus, and used \$30:

* Top-up amount: \$100
* Bonus credits (deducted): \$10
* Used amount: \$30
* Refundable amount: \$100 - \$30 - \$10 = **\$60**
* If paid via WeChat/Alipay: actual refund **\$60** (no fee)
* If paid via PayPal: actual refund \$60 - \$60 × 1% = **\$59.40**

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund after 30 days?">
    Refunds are generally not available after 30 days. We recommend evaluating the service within the first 30 days. For special circumstances, please contact support to discuss options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can an expired balance be refunded?">
    **No.** Effective 2 August 2026, account balances carry a 365-day validity period, and expired balance lapses automatically with **no refund, restoration, or conversion**.

    Any new top-up resets the validity of your entire available balance back to a full year, so a regular top-up and spend cadence is unaffected. The 30-day refund window is shorter than the validity period, so the two do not conflict. See [balance validity](/en/faq/recharge-promotions) for the full rules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why are bonus credits deducted?">
    Promotional bonus credits are complimentary — they are not part of what you actually paid. Refunds only cover the unused portion of your actual payment, so bonus credits are deducted. See [Top-up Promotions](/en/faq/recharge-promotions) for bonus details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I get a refund after an invoice is issued? (Mainland China)">
    In mainland China, once a VAT invoice has been issued, tax processing is involved, making refunds infeasible. We recommend holding off on invoice requests until you're certain you won't need a refund.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I still use my account after a refund?">
    Yes. A refund only returns unused balance — your account and API keys remain active. Simply top up again when you need to continue using the service.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I request a partial refund?">
    Yes. You can choose to refund only part of your balance. Just let support know the amount you'd like refunded.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a refund take?">
    WeChat and Alipay refunds usually arrive within 1-3 business days. Bank transfers, USDT, Wise, and PayPal refunds typically take 3-7 business days. If your refund hasn't arrived after this period, contact support to check the status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
