# How is content safety and compliance ensured?

> APIYI content safety and compliance policy description, including content review mechanism and violation handling measures

## Content Safety Assurance System

APIYI is committed to providing safe and compliant AI services, adopting multi-layered security measures to ensure content compliance.

## Content Review Mechanism

### Professional Review System

We have integrated **professional official content review APIs** to provide real-time protection for all requests:

<Info>
  **Review Working Principle**

  * **Real-time Detection**: Every API request goes through content review
  * **Intelligent Identification**: Automatically identifies potentially violating content
  * **Proactive Interception**: Violating content is not returned to users
  * **Zero Tolerance Policy**: Strict measures for violating content
</Info>

### Review Coverage

* **Input Content Review**: Check all text and images submitted by users
* **Output Content Review**: Ensure AI-generated content complies with standards
* **Multi-language Support**: Support mainstream language review including Chinese and English
* **Multi-modal Detection**: Cover various content forms including text and images

## Compliance Requirements

### User Responsibilities

<Warning>
  **Legal Compliance**

  Users must **comply with local laws and regulations** when using APIYI services, including but not limited to:

  * National and local legal provisions
  * Industry regulatory requirements
  * International legal norms
  * Platform terms of use
</Warning>

### Prohibited Content Types

The following types of content are strictly prohibited:

* **Illegal Content**: Information involving illegal activities
* **Harmful Information**: Content that may cause harm
* **Inappropriate Content**: Information violating social moral standards
* **Infringing Content**: Materials infringing on others' rights

## Violation Handling Measures

### Violation Consequences

APIYI platform reserves the right to take action against violations:

<Card title="Violation Handling Mechanism" icon="gavel">
  **Minor Violations**

  * Content interception and reminder
  * Usage guidance and warning

  **Repeated Violations**

  * Account usage restrictions
  * Feature permission downgrade

  **Serious Violations**

  * Account suspension or termination
  * Paid fees non-refundable
  * Cooperation with relevant authorities for investigation
</Card>
