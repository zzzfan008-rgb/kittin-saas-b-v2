# How do I apply for an agent partnership? Can I earn rebates by inviting friends?

> APIYI offers a built-in referral rebate program — invite friends and earn a continuous 5% credit rebate. No separate agent application required.

## Quick Answer

APIYI does **not** have a separate "agent partnership" application channel. Instead, every user has access to the built-in **referral rebate** program: grab your invite link / invite code from the Account Center, and you'll earn a **continuous 5% credit rebate** on every friend you successfully invite.

<Info>
  Rebates are issued as **in-platform credits** (API usage balance). They **cannot be withdrawn** — they can only be spent on your own API calls.
</Info>

## Where can I find my invite link and invite code?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Account Center">
    After signing in, go directly to the [Account Center / Profile](https://api.apiyi.com/account/profile) page
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locate the &#x22;推广信息 / Referral Info&#x22; section">
    Scroll to the **推广信息 (Referral Info)** card on the page — you'll see your **Invite Code** and an **Invite Link** button
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy your invite link / invite code">
    Click to copy, then share with your friends
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do rebates arrive and get spent?

Each time an invitee **completes a recharge**, your share is credited to your **Reward (奖励金) balance**. You can then **manually transfer the reward into your main API balance**, after which it can be spent on regular API calls.

<Info>
  **Track your rebate history**: in the **Logs** page, filter by **Type = 奖励金划转 (Reward Transfer)** to see each rebate entry along with its source and amount.
</Info>

## What if the invite link loses the referral relationship at signup?

In some browsers, private/incognito modes, or redirected flows, the cookie/parameter from the invite link can be lost, so the referral relationship may not be established automatically. In that case, your friend can bind it manually:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration page">
    Have your friend visit the APIYI signup page
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the &#x22;Invite Code&#x22; field">
    On the registration form, enter your personal invite code in the **Invite Code** field
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete registration">
    Submit the form — the referral relationship will be established successfully
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Recommendation**: When sharing your invite link, **also send your invite code** as a backup, so the relationship can still be linked even if the link parameter gets dropped. Once registration is complete without an invite code, the referral relationship **cannot be added retroactively**.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can rebate credits be withdrawn as cash?">
    **No.** Rebates are issued as **in-platform API usage credits** — for your own use only. They cannot be withdrawn to a bank account, WeChat, or Alipay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the rebate one-time or ongoing?">
    **Ongoing.** As long as the referral relationship is in place, every recharge and spend by your invitee continues to generate a 5% rebate to your account at the current rate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are friends who signed up earlier settled at 10% or 5%?">
    Settlement always follows the **current rule** — rebates earned after **May 15, 2026 (UTC+8)** are calculated at 5%. Rebates already paid before that date are not retroactively adjusted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can enterprise users get a higher rebate rate?">
    **No.** Both enterprise and individual users are settled uniformly at **5%** — no tiered or custom rates are offered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I invite my own alt accounts to farm rebates?">
    **Self-invitation is strictly prohibited.** The platform identifies suspicious accounts via multiple signals including **IP, device fingerprint, and payment information**. If detected:

    * The platform **reserves the right to unilaterally cancel the referral relationship**
    * Any suspicious rebates already paid out **may be reclaimed or frozen**
    * Severe cases may trigger account risk-control actions

    The referral rebate program is designed to reward genuine new-user acquisition — please do not attempt any form of abuse.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I check my invitees and rebate history?">
    Open the [Account Center](https://api.apiyi.com/account/profile) to view your invitee data. For per-transaction rebate entries, go to the **Logs** page and filter by **Type = 奖励金划转 (Reward Transfer)**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
