# Stripe Online Recharge Now Live · Foreign Credit Cards Supported

> APIYI has launched Stripe online recharge with foreign credit card support and strong fraud-prevention controls. Please use a real-name card issued to your company / yourself / your school — malicious payments will trigger account ban.

**2026/5/19 17:01 (UTC+8)** · Service Notice

🎉 **Stripe online recharge now live · foreign credit cards supported** — Good news: APIYI has launched the **Stripe online recharge channel** with **foreign credit card** support and real-time crediting. Stripe enforces strict risk controls and 3D Secure verification — **high security level**, smoother for our overseas customers.

⚠️ **Usage rules (please read)**:

* Only **foreign-currency credit cards** are accepted (CNY cards cannot use the Stripe channel)
* Pay with a **real-name credit card** issued to **your company / yourself / your school**
* **Unsafe or non-real-name cards** will be rejected outright by Stripe's risk controls
* Accounts attempting **malicious payments** (stolen cards, chargebacks, fraud testing, etc.) will be **banned** — do not test or abuse the channel

💡 **How to recharge**: After logging in, head to the balance / recharge page at `api.apiyi.com` and pick the Stripe option. Other payment methods (WeChat / Alipay / bank transfer) are unaffected and remain available.
