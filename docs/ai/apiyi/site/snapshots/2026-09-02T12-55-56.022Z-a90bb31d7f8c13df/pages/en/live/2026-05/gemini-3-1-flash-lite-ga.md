🚀 **Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite goes GA** — Google announced GA on **May 8 (UTC+8)**. The model name drops `-preview` and becomes `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`, with the API contract frozen and production-ready. Output is **64% faster** than 2.5 Flash (381.9 vs 232.3 t/s) and **2.5× faster** on TTFT; GPQA Diamond **86.9%** / MMMU Pro **76.8%**, 1M context + full multimodal input.

💰 **Pricing identical to Google's rates**: \$0.250 / \$1.500 per 1M tokens (cached input \~10% of list). APIYi's official direct-connection channel is live in sync; stacking the top-up promo brings effective cost to **\~79–85% of list**.
