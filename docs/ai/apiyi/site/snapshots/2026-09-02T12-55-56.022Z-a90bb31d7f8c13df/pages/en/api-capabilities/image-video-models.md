## 💰 Pricing Information

* **Pay-as-you-go**: Charged based on actual usage, no minimum charge
* **Balance validity**: Valid for 365 days from the top-up date, automatically reset on the next top-up (see [Recharge Promotions](/en/faq/recharge-promotions))
* Visit [APIYI Console Pricing Page](https://www.apiyi.com/account/pricing) for latest pricing on all models
