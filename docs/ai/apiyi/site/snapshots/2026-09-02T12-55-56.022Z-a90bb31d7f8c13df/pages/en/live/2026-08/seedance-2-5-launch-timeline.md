**2026/8/19 17:51 (UTC+8)** · New Model · ByteDance

📊 **SeeDance 2.5 is not yet live, integration expected to finish by late August**

Before launch, our system first needs to complete compatibility work for the new billing rules — SeeDance 2.5's pricing structure differs from the current `doubao-seedance-2-0` series, and that is the main blocker on the current schedule, not a qualification or review issue.

Exact launch date and pricing will follow an official announcement, and we'll post another update once integration is complete. The full SeeDance 2.0 lineup (standard tier plus the limited-time `SD2Mini` / `SD2Fast` groups) is unaffected and remains available as usual.
