### Balance Validity (effective 2026-08-02)

Account balances are valid for **365 days** from the date of payment. The rules are:

| Item                           | Rule                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Validity period**            | Topped-up credit is valid for 365 days from the date of payment                                    |
| **New top-up**                 | Resets the validity of your **entire available balance** back to a full year                       |
| **Bonus / promotional credit** | Same rule as topped-up credit — 365 days, and likewise reset by a new top-up                       |
| **Existing balances**          | Counted as 365 days from the effective date (2026-08-02), not backdated to historical top-up dates |
| **On expiry**                  | Expired balance lapses automatically — no refund, no restoration, no conversion                    |
| **Checking expiry**            | The expiry date of your current balance is shown in the console                                    |

<Warning>
  **Size your top-ups to your actual usage**: as long as you top up and spend on a regular cadence, each new top-up resets the whole balance back to a full year, so this will not affect you in practice. But if you do not expect to use the credit within a year, do not over-top-up just to reach a higher bonus tier — expired balance is not refunded or restored.

  The authoritative wording is in the [Terms of Service](https://cf-index.apiyi.com/terms) and the [Refund Policy](https://cf-index.apiyi.com/refund).
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can first-time bonus stack with tiered bonus?">
    **Yes, they can stack!**

    Example: First recharge of \$700

    * First-time bonus: \$1 (or \$2 for education users)
    * Tiered bonus: \$700 × 12% = \$84
    * Total credit: \$700 + \$1 + \$84 = **\$785** (regular users)

    Remember to [contact us on Enterprise WeChat](https://work.weixin.qq.com/kfid/kfc9adfd5810ece25ec) to claim the first-time bonus!
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does it take for recharge to arrive?">
    **Arrival Time**:

    * **Online Payment**: Instant (principal amount credited immediately)
    * **Tiered Bonus**: Credited daily between 23:00 - 01:00 (UTC+8) by our finance team (system-automation + human review) — visible the next day
    * **First-Time Bonus**: Manually added after [contacting us on Enterprise WeChat](https://work.weixin.qq.com/kfid/kfc9adfd5810ece25ec), usually within 1 business day

    **Corporate Transfer**: Processed within 1 business day after receipt
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a minimum recharge amount?">
    **No minimum limit**, but recommended:

    * Minimum recharge \$10+ to avoid frequent recharges
    * Recharge \$100+ to enjoy tiered bonus benefits
    * Larger one-time recharge for higher bonus percentages — weigh this against the 365-day validity period
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does recharged balance expire?">
    **Yes.** Effective 2 August 2026, account balances carry a **365-day validity period**:

    * Topped-up credit is valid for one year from the date of payment
    * **Any new top-up resets the validity of your entire available balance** back to a full year, so a regular top-up and spend cadence is unaffected
    * Bonus and promotional credit follow the same rule
    * Existing balances are counted as 365 days from 2026-08-02
    * Expired balance lapses automatically — no refund, no restoration, no conversion

    The expiry date of your current balance is shown in the console. Full terms: [Terms of Service](https://cf-index.apiyi.com/terms) and [Refund Policy](https://cf-index.apiyi.com/refund).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can recharged balance be refunded?">
    According to platform policy:

    * **Unused Recharged Balance**: Refundable in special circumstances
    * **Bonus Balance**: Non-refundable
    * **Used Balance**: Non-refundable

    Contact customer service if you need a refund.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I recharged $600, why only 12% bonus instead of tiered calculation?">
    **Bonus percentage is calculated based on total recharge tier, not cumulative segments.**

    **Correct Calculation**:

    * \$600 recharge falls in \$500 ≤ X \< \$1,000 tier
    * Bonus percentage: 12%
    * Bonus amount: \$600 × 12% = \$72
    * Actual credit: \$672

    **Not Segmented** (incorrect):
    ❌ First \$500 × 12% + Next \$100 × 10%
  </Accordion>

    3. Fill in invoice information (minimum required: company name and tax ID)
