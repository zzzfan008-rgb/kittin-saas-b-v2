**2026/6/19 15:15 (UTC+8)** · Model Status · Google

⚠️ **Nano Banana Pro / 2 blurry at 4K and 2K · 1K still normal · Google-side issue**

Since this morning, `gemini-3-pro-image` (Nano Banana Pro) and `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (Nano Banana 2) have been returning blurry images; by now **4K / 2K are near-constant** while **1K is still fine**. We assess this as a 100% Google-side issue, not an APIYI channel factor.

Other image channels you can use meanwhile (pick as needed, status below):

* `gpt-image-2-vip`: regained its `size` parameter last night, supports 2K / 4K
* official-relay `gpt-image-2`: billed by tokens, same price as OpenAI's official site
* Nano Banana series at 1K: 1K resolution still generates normally

We'll sync an update the moment Google recovers. For consumer-facing products, it's worth integrating multiple models so you can switch at any time.
