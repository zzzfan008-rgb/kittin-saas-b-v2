# sora-2 / sora-2-pro official-relay models to be retired on July 1

> Due to OpenAI shifting compute, these models time out severely, so APIYI will retire maintenance of the sora-2 and sora-2-pro official-relay models on July 1. Although the official EOL is September, they are already frequently unstable; for a better experience, migrate to other video models early — wan-2.7 and SeeDance 2.0 are currently stable.

**2026/6/17 13:39 (UTC+8)** · Service Notice · OpenAI

🎬 **`sora-2` / `sora-2-pro` official-relay models to be retired on July 1** —— Because OpenAI's compute shift causes severe timeouts on these two models, APIYI will retire their maintenance on **July 1 (UTC+8)**. Although the official EOL is September, they are already frequently unstable; for a better user experience, we recommend migrating to other video models early — `wan-2.7` and [SeeDance 2.0](/en/api-capabilities/seedance2/overview) are currently stable.
