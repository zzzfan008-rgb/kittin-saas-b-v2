**2026/6/18 23:33 (UTC+8)** · Model Status · OpenAI

🔥 **Good news! `gpt-image-2-vip` has the `size` parameter back, with 2K and 4K supported** —— A Codex-side rule change between Jun 16 and Jun 18 broke resolution control on `gpt-image-2-vip`.
