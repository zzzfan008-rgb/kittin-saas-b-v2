# Stripe Minimum Top-Up Raised to $50

> Due to Stripe's fee and settlement structure, the minimum top-up amount per transaction via Stripe is now $50, and Stripe is unavailable below that amount. All other payment methods are unchanged, with a $5 minimum.

**2026/7/28 16:44 (UTC+8)** · Service Notice

🗂️ **The minimum top-up per transaction via Stripe is now \$50 — all other payment methods are unchanged**

Why the change: Stripe's fee and settlement structure is unfavorable for small payments — the smaller the transaction, the larger the share taken by fees. Effective immediately, Stripe is only available when the top-up amount is **\$50 or more**.

Minimum top-up by payment method:

* Stripe (foreign-currency credit card): \$50 per transaction
* All other payment methods: \$5 per transaction, unchanged

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your understanding and continued support. — The APIYI Team
